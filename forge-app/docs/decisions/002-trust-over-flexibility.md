# Decision: Trust Over Flexibility


## Problem


Most productivity apps optimize for flexibility — letting users change plans, reschedule tasks, and adjust deadlines constantly. This creates a culture of broken promises where the system becomes a suggestion box rather than a commitment device.


## Options Considered


1. **Maximum flexibility** — Users can change anything, anytime
2. **Guided flexibility** — System suggests alternatives when changes are made
3. **Trust-first design** — System protects commitments and builds trust over time


## Decision


We chose **trust-first design**. Forge prioritizes building trust between the user and themselves. The system is designed to keep promises, not just make them.


## Reasoning


- Trust is built through consistency
- Broken promises erode self-confidence
- The system should model the behavior it wants to see
- Trust increases when the system becomes quieter and more reliable


## Trade-offs


- Less flexibility for users who want to change plans frequently
- May feel rigid for users who are still building habits
- Requires careful onboarding to explain the philosophy


## Future Impact


This decision affects how we handle AI recommendations, plan generation, and user interactions. The system will always ask: "Does this build trust or break it?" before suggesting changes.
