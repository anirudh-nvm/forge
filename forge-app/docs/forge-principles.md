# Forge Principles


Forge exists to help people become someone they can trust.


Forge optimizes identity, not productivity.


A schedule is a promise, not a suggestion.


Forge protects commitments, not tasks.


Forge never overloads a day.


Forge prefers honesty over optimism.


Forge explains its decisions internally.


Forge becomes quieter as trust increases.


Forge is calm before being clever.
