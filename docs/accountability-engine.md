# Accountability Engine

> The system that helps people become someone they can trust.

---

## Purpose

Forge does not measure productivity.

Forge measures trust.

Every interaction, every schedule, every reminder and every reflection exists for one purpose:

Increase the probability that a person keeps the promises they make to themselves.

---

# Core Philosophy

Forge is not a planner.

Forge is not a habit tracker.

Forge is not a motivation app.

Forge is an accountability system.

It learns how a person actually lives, protects the commitments they care about, and gradually rebuilds confidence through consistent execution.

---

# The Trust Model

Forge maintains an internal understanding of how reliable a person's commitments are.

This value is never shown directly.

It exists to help Forge make better decisions.

Trust is influenced by:

• Commitments completed
• Commitments intentionally moved
• Commitments silently ignored
• Planning accuracy
• Recovery after difficult days
• Honesty during reflections

The goal is never perfection.

The goal is reliability.

---

# The Confidence Model

Every morning Forge predicts:

"How likely is today's plan to succeed?"

Confidence is calculated using observations such as:

• Sleep
• Previous day's completion
• Calendar load
• Travel
• Deadlines
• Energy patterns
• Historical behaviour

Forge uses this confidence to decide whether to simplify or protect the day.

---

# The Learning Engine

The first weeks are observation.

Forge learns patterns instead of enforcing routines.

Examples:

• Focus hours
• Sleep schedule
• Meal timing
• Gym consistency
• Study behaviour
• Recovery after failures

Forge never learns bad habits as permanent behaviour.

It identifies them as obstacles.

Example:

Instead of learning

"I usually sleep at 2 AM."

Forge learns

"Late sleep reduces next-day execution."

---

# Daily Analysis

Every morning Forge performs a silent analysis before creating a schedule.

Inputs include:

Today's commitments

Calendar

Deadlines

Travel

Health

Historical behaviour

Previous reflections

Energy patterns

Only after understanding the day does Forge respond.

---

# Guided Planning

Forge explains important decisions.

Example:

Gym
6:00 PM

Suggested because:

• You've completed more sessions at this time.
• Earlier sessions were often skipped.
• You'll already be nearby.

Transparency creates trust.

---

# Risk Prediction

Before the day begins Forge predicts possible weak points.

Example

Today's Risk

DSA

Reason:

You've skipped Tuesday sessions for three weeks.

Let's protect this block.

Prediction is used to support.

Never to discourage.

---

# Recovery Engine

Forge does not punish.

Forge investigates.

Instead of

Missed Task

Forge asks

What happened?

Possible answers:

Ran out of time

Unexpected work

Too tired

Poor planning

Forgot

Not important anymore

Forge then adapts tomorrow's plan.

Failure becomes feedback.

# Interaction Rules

Forge always:

Creates clarity

Protects important commitments

Explains major decisions

Learns from behaviour

Rewards honesty

Adapts plans

Never shames

Never manipulates

Never creates guilt

---

# Success Metric

Forge is successful when users begin saying:

"If I put it into Forge...

I'll probably do it."

Eventually this becomes

"If I tell myself I'll do something...

I know I will."

That is the product.

---

# The Forge Rule

Forge should never speak unless it improves the moment.

Every message must do at least one of these:

• Build trust
• Create clarity
• Encourage action

If a message does none of the above,

Forge stays silent.

Silence is better than noise.
